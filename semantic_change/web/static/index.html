📐 Mathematical Formulations & Measurement Protocols
1. Global Semantic Displacement (Cosine Distance):

For a word w represented at historical time periods t1 and t2 by unit vectors vw(t1) and vw(t2):

Displacement(w, t_1, t_2) = 1 - cos(v_w^(t_1), v_w^(t_2))
2. Second-Order Contextual Drift Cosine Similarity (CDCS):

An invariant, alignment-free measurement. Evaluates the similarity vector against a stable anchor lexicon A = {a_1, ..., a_m}:

Profile^(t)(w) = [cos(v_w^(t), v_(a_i)^(t))] → CDCS(w) = 1 - cos(Profile^(t_1), Profile^(t_2))
3. Local Neighborhood Turnover (Jaccard Distance):

Quantifies the replacement rate of the top k nearest semantic neighbors:

JaccardTurnover(w, t_1, t_2) = 1 - (|NN_k(t_1) ∩ NN_k(t_2)| / |NN_k(t_1) ∪ NN_k(t_2)|)
4. Primary Diachronic Datasets:
  • Google Books Ngram Corpus (1800–2019): Over 8 million digitized books; live normalized annual timeseries.
  • Stanford HistWords (Hamilton et al. 2016): SGNS and SVD multi-decadal vector representations aligned via Orthogonal Procrustes.
  • SemEval-2020 Task 1 (Schlechtweg et al. 2020): Benchmark dataset for unsupervised lexical semantic change detection.
